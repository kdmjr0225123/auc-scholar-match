## What Exists

**Product:** Elevaid matches HBCU students to scholarships they qualify for, automatically. Tagline: "Where access meets opportunity." Business model is B2B — institutions pay ($500–2,000/month, target 107 HBCUs nationally), students never do.

**Status:** Live at elevaid.pro. Soft-launched during Morehouse orientation week. Currently in early growth phase across the AUC (Morehouse, Spelman, Clark Atlanta, Morris Brown) ahead of a wider multi-school rollout.

**Current audience (live numbers, Sept 4, 2026):**

| School | Students |
|---|---|
| Morehouse | 41 |
| Spelman | 8 |
| Clark Atlanta | 4 |
| Morris Brown | 2 |
| **Total** | **55** |

Real, organic signups — not a projection. Concentrated at Morehouse, which is why campus-leader relationships at the other three schools move the needle fastest right now.

**Student experience today:** sign up → short profile (school, major, GPA, grad year) → dashboard of real scholarship matches with award amount, deadline, and a pre-validated link (checked for dead links/CAPTCHA before it reaches a student). Students can mark applications and track status. No ads, no upsell.

**Beta results:**
- 55 students, real usage, matching engine confirmed working end-to-end
- Every registered student currently matches at least 4 live scholarships — monitored automatically, every day
- A pipeline bug that was silently removing live scholarships was caught and fixed this cycle; a daily automated check now exists specifically to catch this kind of issue immediately instead of after the fact

**Current gap:** 4 active scholarships live (the resource this replaces had 150+ listed). This is the number to calibrate outreach around this week.
